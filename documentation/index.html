Money: The Manual

How to install Money, set it up, and keep the records of a self-employed business, part by part. The same help is inside the app: Money > Help Centre.

Nothing in the manual matches. Try one word, such as "mileage" or "VAT".

WWelcome To Money

Money keeps the records of a self-employed business: what comes in, what goes out, and what that means for your tax.

Money is a record-keeping app for UK sole traders. Everything you type in stays in your own copy of the app, on your own website. Nobody else can see it.

What Money Does

  • Keeps every invoice, payment and expense, with receipts attached.
  • Files each cost to the right box of the Self Assessment return, so your accountant (or you) can copy the totals across.
  • Works out your profit, an estimate of your Income Tax and National Insurance, and when to pay it.
  • Reads bank statements and receipt emails, so you type less.
  • Handles mileage, working from home, equipment, stock, VAT and the Construction Industry Scheme when they apply to you.

What Money Does Not Do

  • It does not send returns or payments to HMRC. You file your Self Assessment, VAT and CIS returns yourself, or your accountant does, using the figures here.
  • The tax figures are an estimate to plan with. They follow the rules of each tax year (see Settings > Tax Rules), but your real bill depends on things Money cannot see, such as savings interest or student loans.

Where To Start

  1. Business Setup (Money > Business Setup): pick your kind of work. Money adds categories, income streams and dashboard cards that suit it.
  2. Your details (Settings > Money > Your Business On Invoices): your business name, address and logo for invoices.
  3. Your tax (Settings > Money > Your Tax): where you pay Income Tax, and whether you use the cash basis or traditional accounting.
  4. Start recording: send an invoice, add an expense, or load a bank statement.
Want to look around first? Money > Demo Data adds a year of made-up records, and one button takes them all away again.

IInstalling Money

Money runs on ordinary web hosting with cPanel. It needs no special software on the server.

What The Hosting Needs

  • PHP 8.1 or newer (cPanel > Select PHP Version), with the extensions pdo_mysql, mbstring, openssl, json, ctype and fileinfo. Also switch on gd (images), curl (card payments), zip (update packages) and intl. The installer checks each one and says what to switch on.
  • A MySQL or MariaDB database and a user for it (made in cPanel > MySQL Databases).
  • An SSL certificate for the website (cPanel > SSL/TLS Status, often free). Money should always be opened with https.

Step By Step

  1. In cPanel, make the database and its user, and give the user all privileges on the database. Note the three names and the password.
  2. Upload everything in the package into the folder your domain points to (often public_html): index.php, .htaccess, assets and application side by side.
  3. Make application/storage and application/config writable (755 or 775 in cPanel > File Manager > Permissions).
  4. Open the website. The installer starts by itself: it checks the server, asks for the database details, the site name and the first admin account.
  5. Your Business: pick the kinds of work, how you work (vehicle, working from home, CIS, stock) and, if you like, demo data to try it first. Choose Fresh Start for an empty Money.
  6. Check the summary and press Install. Sign in with the admin account: the getting-started tour explains each part.
The .htaccess file keeps the application folder away from the web. If the host lets you, you can move application one folder above the website: index.php finds it by itself.

UUpdating Money

Each new version comes as an update package (a zip). Updates keep every record.

  1. Take a backup first (see Updates and Backups).
  2. In the admin, open System > Updates and upload the package. It checks the package and installs only what changed.
  3. If the yellow banner asks, press Finish update. It updates the database and gives the Administrator any new permissions.

Updates can also be copied over the site by FTP: never delete application/config or application/storage. Then open the admin and press Finish update.

Install updates in order. Each package says which version it updates from.

TTasks That Run By Themselves

Some things happen on a schedule: repeating invoices and expenses, invoice reminders, checking the mailbox for receipts, scheduled reports and the weekly digest.

  • Nothing is required: the built-in scheduler runs due tasks after normal page views.
  • For reliable timing, add a cron job in cPanel > Cron Jobs, once a minute: /usr/local/bin/php /home/USER/public_html/ap schedule:run (change the path to your site), or call the secret web-cron address shown on System > Scheduled tasks from a free cron service.

System > Scheduled tasks lists each task, when it last ran and what it did.

PPrivacy and Security

  • Your records live only in your own copy of Money, in your own database on your own hosting. Nothing is sent to anyone else, and no one else holds a copy.
  • Passwords for email, Stripe and PayPal are stored encrypted with a key kept in application/config.
  • Receipts and files are kept in application/storage, which the web cannot open directly; they are shown only to people signed in with permission.
  • Clients see only their own invoices, through private links that cannot be guessed.
  • Give each person their own account with the access they need (Users > Roles and access), and switch on two-step sign-in.
  • Take backups of the database and application/storage, and keep one away from the website.

BBusiness Setup

Ready-made categories, income streams, ways of paying and dashboard cards for your kind of work.

Money > Business Setup

Business Setup gives you a starting set of records that suits what you do. It only ever adds: anything you already have with the same name is left exactly as it is, so you can run it again at any time, for example when you take on a second kind of work.

  1. Tick one or more kinds of work: Driver Or Courier, Tradesperson Or Builder, Freelancer Or Creative, Retail Or Online Seller, Consultant Or Professional, Hair and Beauty, Cleaning and Home Services, or Something Else.
  2. Say how you work: whether you use a vehicle (this shows Vehicles and the Mileage Log), work from home, are paid under CIS, or sell goods from stock.
  3. Look at What Will Be Added and untick anything you do not want.
  4. Press Add To My Records.

Every category is filed to a box of the Self Assessment return (for example "Car, van and travel expenses"). You can rename, refile or delete any of them later on Money > Categories.

DDemo Data

A year of made-up records to try Money out, taken away again with one button.

Money > Demo Data

Demo data fills every page with something to look at: clients and suppliers, invoices (some paid, some still open), payments, five to eight expenses a month, a vehicle with a mileage log, a laptop as equipment, and stock items if you sell goods.

  • Made-up names end in "(Demo)" and demo invoices are numbered DEMO-0001 and on. Your own invoice numbers carry on as if they were not there.
  • While demo data is in use, the dashboard shows a banner with Delete All Demo Data.
  • Deleting takes away exactly what was added. If one of your own records uses a demo client, supplier, category or stream, that one is kept.
Delete the demo data before you start keeping real records, so your totals are your own.

TThe Dashboard

Your year at a glance: money in, costs, profit, tax to put aside, and anything that needs a look.

Dashboard (the first page after signing in)

The dashboard shows one tax year at a time (6 April to 5 April). Change the year with the Tax year box at the top.

The Cards

  • The year: income, allowable costs, profit and the estimated tax, with where the year is heading at the current pace.
  • Quarters: the four quarterly updates of Making Tax Digital. Mark a quarter as sent and add a note.
  • Months: open a month to see every payment and cost behind it.
  • Needs A Look: overdue invoices, expenses with no category or no receipt, VAT or CIS returns due, stock running low, and more.
  • Invoices, latest payments and expenses, income streams and deadlines.

Arranging The Cards

  1. Press Customise. Drag a card to move it, or use the arrows, and choose a width.
  2. Press Add A Card to build your own: a number, a chart or a list, for any measure, period and category.
  3. Changes save by themselves. Reset Layout puts the ready-made layout back.

IInvoices

Create invoices, email them with a pay link, and see what is still owed.

Money > Invoices

Making An Invoice

  1. Press New Invoice. The number counts up by itself (change the start in Settings > Money).
  2. Choose the client (or type a new name), the bill date and the due date, and the income stream it belongs to.
  3. Save, then press Add Item for each thing you charge for: a title, quantity, unit and rate. Drag items to reorder them.
  4. Add a discount if you give one. With VAT or CIS, choose the rate on each line (see the VAT and CIS articles).

Sending It

  • Actions > Email Invoice sends it with a Pay Invoice button. The client's private page shows the invoice and lets them pay by card (Stripe), PayPal, or bank transfer with your details. Set these up in Settings > Invoice Payments.
  • View PDF and Save As PDF give a printable invoice. Its look (style, colour, font, wording) is in Settings > Invoice PDF Look.
  • Send Reminder appears once an invoice is overdue or was emailed and is still unpaid.

Statuses

  • Unpaid nothing paid yet; Overdue unpaid after the due date; Part Paid some paid; Paid paid in full.
  • Draft invoices are not counted anywhere until you mark them as sent. Cancelled ones are kept for your records but never counted.

Repeating Invoices

Tick Repeat this invoice automatically on the invoice form, choose how often and when to stop. Each new invoice gets the next number and a copy of the items, and can be emailed to the client by itself.

Clients and Statements

On Money > Clients and Suppliers, each client has a statement of invoices and payments, which you can email, and a private portal link where they see all their invoices.

PPayments

Every bit of money that comes in, whether it pays an invoice or not.

Money > Payments

Record every payment you receive. Your income for tax is the total of your payments in the tax year (on the cash basis).

  1. Press New Payment.
  2. If it pays an invoice, choose the invoice: the client, stream and amount are filled in for you, and the invoice's balance goes down.
  3. Otherwise say where it came from (for example "Weekly platform payout"), the date, amount, stream and how it was paid.
  4. Save. With VAT, CIS or Stock switched on, the extra boxes appear for a payment with no invoice.

Payments made through the pay link (Stripe or PayPal) are added by themselves, once each.

Tick several payments in the list to change their stream or client together, or delete them.

EExpenses

Your business costs, each filed to the right box of the tax return, with the receipt kept.

Money > Expenses

  1. Press New Expense, say what it was for, the date and the amount.
  2. Choose a category. The category decides the box of the tax return, and whether the cost counts at all (pension payments and personal spending do not).
  3. Set Business use (%) if it is partly personal, for example 60 for a phone used 60% for work.
  4. Drop the receipt (photo or PDF) on the form.
  5. Tick This cost comes again for rent, insurance or subscriptions: a copy is made each period until the date or number you choose.

Good To Know

  • Things you buy to keep (tools, a laptop, a van) go to a category filed to "Equipment and vehicles bought": they appear on Money > Equipment and are claimed there.
  • Costs for a vehicle on the mileage rate are left out, because the rate covers them (parking and tolls still count).
  • The dashboard reminds you about expenses with no category, and receipts missing on costs over the amount you choose in Settings > Money.
  • Tick several expenses to change their category, stream, vehicle, business share or VAT together.

RReceipt Inbox

Take photos of receipts on your phone now, and add the details later.

Money > Receipt Inbox

  1. On your phone, open Receipt Inbox and press Take a photo. It saves as soon as the photo is taken.
  2. Later, open each receipt and fill in the date, amount and category. It becomes an expense with the photo attached.
Add the admin to your phone's home screen (Share > Add to Home Screen) so the camera is one tap away.

BBank Import

Load a statement from your online banking and turn its lines into expenses and payments.

Money > Bank Import

Loading A Statement

  1. In your online banking, download a statement as CSV (sometimes called "spreadsheet" or "Excel") or OFX/QFX ("Money" or "Quicken").
  2. Drop it on Load A Statement and say which account it is (use the same name each time).
  3. For a CSV from a new bank, Money finds the date, description and amount by their names. If it cannot, it asks you once which column is which and how dates are written; save the layout and next time it is automatic.
  4. Review every line: money out becomes an expense, money in a payment. Change titles and categories, or set the category of all ticked lines at once.
  5. Press Add The Ticked Lines.

Never Twice

  • Each line gets a fingerprint. Lines added from an earlier statement show as Already Added and can never be added again.
  • A line with the same amount as something you typed in by hand, a few days either side, shows as Check and is left unticked. Change how many days in Settings > Money > Bank Import.
  • Money coming in that matches what is left on an open invoice is counted against that invoice.
  • Made a mistake? Recent Imports > Take Back moves everything one import added to the bin.

Rules

Add a rule for each regular payee: when a description contains your words (for example "SHELL"), the line gets that category, stream and title. Drag rules into order: the first that matches wins.

EEmail Import

Receipt and invoice emails become expenses by themselves, with the email or PDF kept.

Settings > Email Import, then Money > Email Import

Connecting Your Mailbox

  1. Open Settings > Email Import and press the button for your provider (Gmail, Yahoo, iCloud, AOL, Zoho, Fastmail, IONOS), or type your provider's IMAP server, port and security.
  2. Make an app password in your email account's security settings (Gmail: Google Account > Security > 2-Step Verification > App passwords). Type your email address and that app password.
  3. Press Test Connection on Money > Email Import.
  4. Switch on Check The Mailbox By Itself Every Hour if you want it to run alone.
Outlook, Hotmail and Microsoft 365 no longer allow other apps to sign in with a password. Set a rule in Outlook that forwards receipts to another mailbox, and connect that one.

Rules

  • A rule says which emails to take (sender, words in the subject, a PDF attached), where the amount is (after words like "Total"), and what the expense should be: title, category, supplier and how it was paid.
  • Press Add Example Rules for four starters: parking receipts, supplier invoices with a PDF, software subscriptions and fuel receipts.
  • Only emails that match a rule are touched. Matching emails can be moved to the bin after they are added.
  • Emails no rule took, and ones that need a look, wait in Emails Without A Rule; Create Rule turns one into a rule with its details filled in.

CClients and Suppliers

The people and businesses you work with, their statements and their private portal.

Money > Clients and Suppliers

  • Add a contact with its name, kind (client or supplier), email, phone and address. Invoices use the client's address.
  • Statement lists a client's invoices and payments with what is still owed. Email one client's statement, or everyone who owes money at once.
  • Portal gives the client a private link to see all their sent invoices and pay them.
  • Switch a contact off to archive it: it is kept on old records but no longer offered on new ones.
  • With CIS on, mark a client as a contractor (with the rate they take off) or a supplier as a subcontractor you pay.

CCategories and Income Streams

Categories decide where each cost goes on your tax return; streams split your income by kind of work.

Money > Categories, and Money > Income Streams

Each expense category is filed to one box of the Self Assessment return, such as "Car, van and travel expenses" or "Phone, fax, stationery and other office costs". Two boxes are never costs: pension payments and personal spending. "Equipment and vehicles bought" goes to Money > Equipment.

Income streams (for example "Client Projects" and "Royalties") show where your money comes from on the dashboard and in reports. Costs can belong to a stream too, so each stream shows its own profit.

Drag categories and streams into the order you want them offered.

EEquipment

Things you buy to keep, and what each tax year can claim for them.

Money > Equipment

Anything filed to "Equipment and vehicles bought" appears here by itself. You can also add equipment by hand. How it is claimed depends on How Your Accounts Are Kept (Settings > Money > Your Tax):

  • Cash basis: equipment is claimed in full, by its business share, in the year you buy it. Cars still get capital allowances.
  • Traditional accounting: the Annual Investment Allowance covers most purchases in full; above it, new main-rate equipment gets the 40% first year allowance, and the rest goes into the main or special rate pool and is claimed a share each year.

Cars are pooled by their CO2: new zero-emission cars get 100% in the first year, up to the limit the main pool, above it the special rate pool. A car used privately gets its own pool, claimed by the business share.

When you sell or give away something, enter the date and what you got: the sale comes off the pool (or counts as income on the cash basis).

A car or van that has had capital allowances can no longer use the mileage rate. Money stops it for you.

VVehicles and Mileage

Claim the mileage rate or the actual running costs, and keep a log of business trips.

Money > Vehicles, and Money > Mileage Log

  1. Add each vehicle you use for work and choose Mileage rate or Actual costs. Once you choose the mileage rate for a vehicle, HMRC expects you to keep it for as long as you use that vehicle.
  2. Log business trips on the Mileage Log: date, miles, from, to and why.
  3. Each vehicle's page compares what the mileage rate gives with its actual costs.

The mileage rates (per mile for the first 10,000 business miles and after) are in Settings > Tax Rules for each year. On the mileage rate, fuel, insurance and repairs are covered by the rate and left out of costs; parking and tolls still count.

No vehicle for work? Switch off Vehicles and Mileage in Settings > Money > Features: the menu items go and nothing is deleted.

WWorking From Home

Claim a share of your household bills, or HMRC's flat rate by the hours you work at home.

Settings > Money > Working From Home

  • Actual costs: file household bills to categories in the "Use of home" box and set the business share on each.
  • Flat rate: HMRC's simplified expenses give a set amount a month by the hours you work at home (from 25 hours a month). Set your hours on the Tax Estimate page for each tax year. Bills filed to "Use of home" are then left out, as the flat rate covers them.

TTax Estimate

Income Tax and National Insurance worked out from your profit, and when to pay them.

Money > Tax Estimate

  • Profit: your income less allowable costs, or less the £1,000 trading allowance if that leaves a lower profit (choose in Settings > Money > Your Tax).
  • Income Tax band by band, with the rates of the tax year, for England, Wales and Northern Ireland or Scotland.
  • National Insurance: Class 4 on profits above the lower limit. Class 2 is no longer charged, but you can add voluntary Class 2 when profits are below the small profits threshold, to keep the year counting for your State Pension.
  • When To Pay: the balance on 31 January after the tax year, and payments on account (half of the bill each, in January and July) when HMRC asks for them.
  • Making Tax Digital: whether it is likely to apply, from your income two tax years before.

Your Other Figures

Add wages or a pension and the tax already taken off them, payments on account you have made, and your hours at home (flat rate). They save in the background and the estimate updates straight away.

This is a planning figure. Your Self Assessment return is the real thing; check it with your accountant.

VVAT

VAT on invoices and costs, the nine boxes of each return, and a watch on the registration threshold.

Settings > VAT, then Money > VAT

Not Registered

Money > VAT watches your takings over the last 12 months against the registration threshold, and the dashboard warns you at the point you choose (80% to start). You must register within 30 days of the end of a month in which your last 12 months go over the threshold, or straight away if you expect the next 30 days alone to go over it.

Registered

  1. In Settings > VAT, tick I Am VAT Registered, add your VAT number and the date you registered, and choose your VAT periods.
  2. Choose your scheme: Standard (pay the VAT you charge, less the VAT on your costs) or Flat Rate (a set percentage of your takings with VAT, no VAT back on costs except equipment of £2,000 or more). Tick cash accounting if you use it.
  3. Invoice lines get a VAT rate; expenses and payments with no invoice get a rate and amount (left empty, the amount is worked out from the total).
  4. Money > VAT shows each period with its nine boxes, what to pay or reclaim, the due date, and every sale and cost behind the figures. Type the boxes into your VAT software, then press Mark As Filed.

Profit leaves VAT out everywhere once you are registered. If records in a filed period change, the page shows what changed.

Construction work under CIS between VAT-registered businesses usually uses the domestic reverse charge: choose "Reverse Charge" on the line and the invoice says the customer accounts for the VAT.

CCIS For Builders

The Construction Industry Scheme, for subcontractors who are paid under it and contractors who pay others.

Settings > CIS, then Money > CIS

Paid Under CIS (Subcontractor)

  1. Tick Contractors Pay Me Under CIS and choose the rate they take off: 20% (registered), 30% (not registered) or nothing (gross payment status).
  2. Mark the client as a contractor on Clients and Suppliers. New invoices to them pick the rate.
  3. On each invoice line, say Labour or Materials. The deduction is on labour only, never materials or VAT. The invoice shows it and asks for the total less the deduction.
  4. For payments with no invoice, type the CIS taken off and the materials from the contractor's statement.
  5. On Money > CIS, tick each contractor's monthly statement when it arrives. Money checks the rate taken.

Your income is the full amount before the deduction, and the deductions come off your tax on the Tax Estimate as tax already paid. If more was taken than you owe, HMRC pays it back after your return.

Paying Subcontractors (Contractor)

  1. Tick I Pay Subcontractors Under CIS and add your employer's reference.
  2. Mark each subcontractor on Clients and Suppliers with the rate and their UTR (and verification number if they are taxed at 30%).
  3. On the expense paying them, type what you paid, the CIS you took off and the materials. The cost is what you paid plus the deduction.
  4. Money > CIS shows each tax month (6th to 5th): print each subcontractor's payment and deduction statement, pay HMRC what you took off by the 22nd, file your monthly return by the 19th, and mark it as filed.

SStock

For goods you buy to sell: what is left, what it cost, and the stock count.

Settings > Money > Features > Stock, then Money > Stock

  1. Add each item with its sell price, how many you have now and what each cost.
  2. When you buy more, add Stock Bought lines to the expense: each item comes in at its cost each, or a share of the amount by quantity.
  3. Invoice lines can come From Stock (picking the item fills in its name and price), and payments with no invoice have Sold From Stock lines.
  4. Use Add Or Take Out for damaged, used or returned items, and Stock Count to put the records right from what is on the shelf.

Costs follow the average of what you paid. With traditional accounting, count your stock on the last day of the tax year: the change in stock value over the year changes your cost of goods. On the cash basis goods count when you pay for them, so stock is for your own use.

RReports

Totals for the tax year, downloads for your accountant, and reports emailed on a schedule.

Money > Reports, and Money > Scheduled Reports

  • Month by month income, costs and profit; costs by tax box and category; income by client; money still owed by age; this year against last.
  • Downloads: payments, expenses, invoices, mileage, income by client and money owed, as CSV files that open in any spreadsheet. With VAT or CIS on, they include those columns.
  • Accountant Pack: every download plus a summary page, in one zip, to email or hand over.
  • Scheduled Reports: choose a report, how often and who receives it, as a PDF or CSV.

CCustom Fields

Add your own boxes to invoices, payments, expenses and contacts.

Money > Custom Fields

Add a box such as "Job number" or "Purchase order" to any form: text, a number, a date, a yes or no, or a choice from a list. Choose whether it is needed, and whether it shows in lists and reports.

SSettings

Where everything is set up: your business, your tax, features, and each part of Money.

Settings

  • Money: your business on invoices, features (vehicles, stock), your tax (where you pay, cash or traditional, trading allowance, Class 2), working from home, quarters, the dashboard and bank import.
  • Tax Rules: the rates and limits of each tax year. Years HMRC has published are built in; change any figure and it is kept for that year only. A new year uses the year before until you copy it in and check it.
  • VAT, CIS and Email Import: see their own articles.
  • Invoice Payments: Stripe, PayPal and bank details, emails and reminders.
  • Invoice PDF Look: style, colour, font, wording and which parts show.
Type any word in the search at the top of the admin (for example "mileage" or "VAT number") to jump to the right page or setting.

UUsers and Access

Let a bookkeeper or accountant in, with only the access they need.

Users > Staff, and Users > Roles and access

  • Two switches decide what someone can do in Money: See income, expenses, invoices and receipts, and Add, change and delete them. Settings need their own permission.
  • Give an accountant a role with view only, or a bookkeeper view and edit.
  • Switch on two-step sign-in under your profile for extra safety.

UUpdates and Backups

Keep Money up to date and your records safe.

System > Updates

  1. Upload the update package on System > Updates (or copy its files over your site).
  2. When the yellow banner asks, press Finish update. It updates the database and keeps every record.

Take a backup of the database and the application/storage folder (receipts and files) regularly, from your hosting control panel. Keep one copy away from your website.

QQuestions and Answers

Answers to the questions people ask most.

Does Money send anything to HMRC?

No. Money keeps your records and works out the figures. You file your returns yourself, or your accountant does.

Why is my profit different from my bank balance?

Profit counts only business income and allowable costs. Money you put in or take out for yourself, pension payments, personal spending and equipment (claimed separately) are not part of it.

An invoice shows as overdue but it was paid.

Add the payment on the invoice (Add Payment), or choose the invoice when you record the payment. A bank import can do it for you when the amount matches.

The bank import added a line I had already typed in.

Lines that look like records added by hand are marked Check and left unticked; if one was added anyway, delete either copy. Take Back on Recent Imports removes a whole import.

Email Import says the sign-in was refused.

Most providers need an app password, not your normal password, and some need IMAP switched on in their settings. Outlook, Hotmail and Microsoft 365 do not allow password sign-in at all: forward receipts to another mailbox instead.

Next year's tax rates are not there.

On Settings > Tax Rules press Add for the new year: it copies the year before for you to check against gov.uk, and says so until you save it.

Can I use Money for two businesses?

Income streams split income and costs within one business. Two separate businesses are best kept in two copies of Money.

WWords Used

Tax year
6 April to 5 April. 2026-27 runs from 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027.
Allowable costs
Business costs that come off your income before tax. Personal spending, pension payments and equipment (claimed separately) are not among them.
Cash basis
Income and costs count when the money comes in or goes out. HMRC's starting choice for sole traders.
Traditional accounting
Income and costs count when they are earned or due, with capital allowances for equipment and stock counted at the year end.
Trading allowance
£1,000 a year you can take off your income instead of your real costs.
Class 4 National Insurance
Paid on profits above the lower limit, through Self Assessment.
Payments on account
Two advance payments towards next year's tax, each half of this year's bill, due 31 January and 31 July.
Making Tax Digital (MTD)
Quarterly updates to HMRC through compatible software, for those whose income is over the limit.
Capital allowances
What can be claimed for equipment and vehicles bought to keep: the Annual Investment Allowance, first year allowances and writing down allowances.
VAT registration threshold
The takings over 12 months at which you must register for VAT.
Flat Rate Scheme
A VAT scheme where you pay a set percentage of your takings with VAT, and do not reclaim VAT on most costs.
CIS
The Construction Industry Scheme: contractors take tax off what they pay subcontractors for building work and pay it to HMRC.
Domestic reverse charge
For CIS building work between VAT-registered businesses, the customer accounts for the VAT instead of the supplier.
IMAP
The way email programs read a mailbox. Money uses it to read receipt emails.
